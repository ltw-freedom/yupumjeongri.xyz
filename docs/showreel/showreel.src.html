대한유품정리 (yupumjeongri.xyz) is an estate-cleanup service for Seoul and Gyeonggi that publishes its prices before anyone asks. Its design system, 정직한 견적서 (the honest ledger), borrows the look of a paper estimate: ruled lines, dotted leaders, a gold seal. Every frame of this reel is computed from a single time value, so you can scrub it frame by frame.
